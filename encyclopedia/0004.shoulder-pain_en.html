Shoulder Pain When Raising Your Arm or Reaching Behind Your Back: Correcting Thoracic Kyphosis to Open Up the Shoulder

Key Summary from Bodyall Korean Medicine Clinic

The structural cause of shoulder pain often lies in the alignment changes of the 'kyphotic thoracic spine,' which holds the shoulder from behind.

When the upper back rounds forward, it generates 'tension' that pulls the shoulder muscles taut, triggering inflammation and adhesion formation.

Bodyall secures spinal space using 'Biomechanical Spatial Spinal Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol)' and resolves adhesions through acupotomy and physical therapy.

1. Do You Struggle With These Movements?

Shoulder disorders are not simply characterized by pain alone; a reduced range of joint motion is a defining feature. Reviewing the following symptoms is recommended.

  • Sharp pain occurs at a specific angle when raising the arm overhead.
  • Movements such as clasping hands behind the back or zipping up a dress are impossible.
  • Lying on the affected shoulder at night intensifies pain, disrupting sleep.
  • The shoulder feels stone-hard and stiff, with the neck also constantly feeling tight.

2. Mechanism Analysis: Twisting an Arm While the Sleeve Is Pulled Taut Tears the Fabric

Moving the arm while the upper back is rounded creates a mechanical situation similar to forcibly rotating an arm while someone grips the sleeve tightly from behind.

• Cause (Kyphotic Upper Back): A rounded upper back tilts the scapula forward, pulling the muscles taut and generating tension.
• Result (Pain): Taut muscles undergo friction easily even with small movements, leading to inflammation (frozen shoulder, tears) much like a seam bursting open.

4. A Solution Aimed at Improving the Fundamental Structural Causes of Degenerative Spinal Conditions

Structural alignment that releases the 'hand pulling from the side' (tension) must precede other steps to establish a biomechanical conservative treatment alternative that can be safely considered prior to surgery.

Resolving Structural Causes: Bodyall's Specialized Treatments

• Biomechanical Spatial Spinal Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) / Chuna Manual Therapy: Biomechanical Spatial Spinal Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) is a biomechanical conservative correction technique grounded in the principles of traditional Osteopathy and Chuna manual therapy, designed to secure physical space between spinal segments. Since the shoulder joint is directly influenced by the alignment of the thoracic and cervical spine, correcting a kyphotic upper back and misaligned spine stably restores the physical space necessary for shoulder movement.
• Acupotomy Therapy (Adhesion Release): This technique micro-dissects stiffened joint capsules and adherent tissue to immediately restore mobility. According to a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials, acupotomy therapy demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) pain scores and improvement in the Constant-Murley Score (CMS) among patients with shoulder adhesive capsulitis compared to control groups[1]. This evidence aligns academically with the mechanical principle of dissecting tension-induced adhesions caused by thoracic kyphosis, and no statistically significant difference in adverse events was observed between groups, supporting the safety profile of this intervention.

5. Self-Management Guide for Daily Life

  • Pendulum Exercise: Bend the upper body forward, let the affected arm hang down, and gently swing it in a circular motion.
  • Towel Stretch: Hold a towel with both hands and use the healthy arm to slowly pull the affected arm upward.
