Shoulder & Arm Self-Diagnosis

"Shoulder pain that makes it hard to lift or reach back, straighten your back to open your shoulders"

💡 30-Second Key Summary from Bodyall Clinic

1. The real cause of shoulder pain lies in the 'curved back (thoracic spine)' that holds the shoulder from behind.

2. A curved back creates 'tension' in the shoulder muscles, leading to inflammation and adhesion.

3. Bodyall straightens the back with SART correction and resolves adhesions with Acupotomy and physical therapy.

1. Do you find these movements difficult?

Shoulder disorders are characterized not just by pain, but by a decrease in the range of motion of the joint. Check your symptoms below.

  • Sharp pain at certain angles when lifting your arm up.
  • Unable to put hands behind your back or reach for a back zipper.
  • Pain worsens when lying on the affected shoulder, interfering with sleep.
  • Shoulder feels as hard as a rock, and the neck is constantly stiff.

2. Mechanism Analysis: "If you rotate your arm while pulling the sleeve, the cloth tears"

Moving your arm while your back is hunched is like someone grabbing your sleeve from behind while you try to force your arm to rotate.

• Cause (Curved Back): A hunched back pulls the shoulder blades forward, stretching the muscles tight (creating tension).
• Result (Pain): Tense muscles easily experience friction during even small movements, leading to inflammation (Frozen Shoulder, tear) like a fraying thread.

3. Distinguishing Frozen Shoulder vs. Rotator Cuff Disease

Category Frozen Shoulder Rotator Cuff Injury
Pain Pattern Limited movement in all directions Pain only at specific angles
Cause Capsular adhesion and aging Repetitive friction and tearing
Main Treatment Acupotomy + Structural Alignment SART Correction + Bee Venom

4. Bodyall's Total Care Solution

Structural alignment, which releases the pulling hand (tension), must precede for fundamental treatment.

① Bodyall Specialized Care: Structural Relief

• SART / Chuna Therapy: The shoulder joint is directly affected by the alignment of the thoracic and cervical spine. We secure physical space for the shoulder to move by correcting the hunched back.
• Acupotomy (Adhesion Release): Minimally separates the hardened joint capsule and adhered tissues to immediately restore mobility.

② Bodyall Comprehensive Basis: Inflammation Control

• Comprehensive Solution: Acupuncture and Bee Venom Therapy remove deep inflammation, while cupping and moxibustion promote blood circulation.
• Modern PT: ICT and TENS relax tense muscles and stabilize pain nerves.

5. Self-Care Guide for Daily Life

  • Pendulum Exercise: Lean forward and let the painful arm hang down, then gently swing it in circles.
  • Towel Stretch: Hold a towel with both hands and slowly pull the affected arm up using the healthy arm.
