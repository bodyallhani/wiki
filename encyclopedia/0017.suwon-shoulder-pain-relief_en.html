1. Biomechanical Misunderstandings of Frozen Shoulder and Radiating Pain

When many people experience worsening shoulder pain and arm numbness, they often assume it is simply 'frozen shoulder' (adhesive capsulitis) or a 'rotator cuff tear.' In reality, displacement of the cervical spine often maximizes tension in the muscles around the shoulder, compressing nerves and radiating pain. Spinal twisting changes the position of the scapula, physically limiting the range of motion of the shoulder joint.

2. Why is shoulder-only treatment limited?

Treating only shoulder inflammation may provide temporary pain relief, but the structural causes in the spine and pelvis that make the shoulder slump remain. Bodyall uses SART (Spinal Alignment Restoration Therapy) to simultaneously correct misalignments in the spine and pelvis, normalizing the nerve and blood vessel pathways leading to the shoulder.
