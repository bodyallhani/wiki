1. Biomechanical Mechanisms Underlying Frozen Shoulder and Radiating Pain

When individuals encounter escalating shoulder immobility alongside upper extremity numbness, clinical focus often defaults exclusively to adhesive capsulitis or localized rotator cuff pathology. However, comprehensive biomechanical assessments reveal that underlying structural displacements within the cervical spine frequently maximize compensatory tension across the pericapsular musculature and impinge descending nerve roots, causing distinct radiating pain[1]. This multi-segmental spinal twisting alters the resting spatial position of the scapulothoracic joint, creating secondary mechanical blocks that restrict the functional range of motion of the glenohumeral joint.

2. Structural Limitations of Isolated Localized Shoulder Interventions

Addressing localized shoulder inflammation via isolated joint therapies may offer temporary symptomatic relief, but the foundational kinetic distortions within the spine and pelvis that perpetuate shoulder slumping and internal rotation remain unaddressed. Bodyall utilizes SART (Spinal Alignment Restoration Therapy)—a conservative corrective technique that secures physical space between spinal segments, based on the principles of traditional osteopathy and Chuna manual therapy. By executing simultaneous structural corrections from the pelvic foundation up to the cervical segments, this approach is a comprehensive solution aimed at improving the fundamental structural causes of degenerative spinal diseases and normalizing the neurovascular pathways supplying the upper extremities[2].
