1. Biomechanical Causes of Arm Numbness and Scapular Pain

The cervical spine acts as the primary architectural support for the cranial mass. When sagittal alignment degrades into a forward head posture, the mechanical load on the lower cervical segments escalates exponentially. Consequently, this leads to debilitating radiating pain propagating down the upper extremities, dermatomal numbness, and persistent distress within the interscapular region[1].
