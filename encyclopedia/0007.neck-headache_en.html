Neck & Shoulder Self-Diagnosis

"Persistent headaches: The cause isn't your head, it's your 'neck'"

💡 30-Second Key Summary from Bodyall Clinic

1. Chronic headaches that don't respond to painkillers are likely caused by upper cervical misalignment.

2. Straight or turtle neck positions compress suboccipital nerves, causing eye strain, migraines, and dizziness.

3. Bodyall restores the C-curve with SART and releases compressed nerves with Acupotomy.

1. Symptoms Painkillers Can't Solve

'Cervicogenic headaches' occur when the alignment of neck vertebrae is compromised. Look for these signs:

  • Stiffness in the back of the neck that travels up to the crown or temples.
  • Pain is primarily one-sided and can involve eye pain or blurred vision.
  • Pressure on the hollow spot where the skull meets the neck recreates the headache.
  • Headaches worsen after prolonged use of computers or smartphones.

2. Mechanism: How Turtle Neck Creates Headaches

When the natural C-curve of the neck is lost, the weight of the head concentrates on the upper cervical spine. Small muscles below the skull then tighten and squeeze the nerves.

• Occipital Nerve Compression: Hardened muscles press against the occipital nerves, creating a stinging or throbbing sensation.
• Blood Flow Obstruction: Tension in neck muscles hinders blood circulation to the brain, leading to chronic fatigue and dizziness.

4. Recommended: Cervical Hygiene Stretch

This is the safest stretch to open up the front of the neck and restore the cervical curve.

  • Cervical Hygiene Stretch: Interlock your fingers and place them at the base of your skull. Open your chest and slowly tilt your head back, holding for 5–10 seconds.
