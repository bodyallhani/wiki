1. Is it Carpal Tunnel Syndrome? Self-Diagnosis

Many patients visiting Bodyall Suwon complain of 'numbness.' Check if any of the following apply to you:

  • My hands tingle and go numb at night, disturbing sleep, and only feel better after shaking them.
  • I feel numbness or loss of sensation from my thumb to half of my ring finger.
  • My grip suddenly weakens, making me drop cups or struggle with buttoning shirts.
  • Tapping the palm side of my wrist sends an electric shock-like sensation to my fingertips.

2. Mechanism: Why do the fingers go numb?

There is a narrow 'tunnel' in the wrist through which the median nerve passes. Repetitive use can cause ligaments to thicken, narrowing this tunnel and squeezing the nerve. Notably, poor cervical alignment (like Turtle Neck) makes the nerve roots sensitive, worsening wrist symptoms.
