Could Your Wrist Condition Be Carpal Tunnel Syndrome?

  • Numbness and tingling in the hands at night disrupt sleep.
  • Sensation is dull or numb from the thumb to half of the ring finger.
  • Sudden loss of grip strength causes objects, such as a cup, to be dropped.

Mechanism Analysis: Why Does the Numbness Extend to the Fingers?

The carpal tunnel, the passage through which the median nerve travels inside the wrist, narrows and irritates the nerve. In particular, when cervical spine alignment is compromised, such as with forward head posture, imbalances in the upper extremity kinetic chain can sensitize the nerve roots further, exacerbating symptoms.
