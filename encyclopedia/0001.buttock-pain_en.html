"My butt tingles like electricity whenever I drive" Hidden Causes of Buttock Pain

💡 30-Second Key Summary from Bodyall Clinic

1. Piriformis Syndrome is pain caused by buttock muscles compressing the sciatic nerve.

2. Similar to a herniated disc, but the main symptom is buttock and leg numbness rather than back pain.

3. For fundamental relief, structural alignment of the misaligned pelvis is essential.

1. Is it just muscle pain? Self-Diagnosis Checklist

Does your back not hurt much, but you suffer from numbness extending from your buttock down your leg? If 3 or more apply, suspect Piriformis Syndrome.

  • My butt tingles when sitting for 30+ minutes for driving or work.
  • Pain worsens when sitting cross-legged or crossing legs.
  • There is a spot in the center of the painful buttock that hurts enough to scream when pressed.
  • It is hard to change positions at night due to pain.
  • My buttock pulls when climbing stairs or walking uphill.

2. Why does this pain occur?

Deep in the buttock, there is a muscle called the 'Piriformis'. Our body's large sciatic nerve passes right under this muscle. If the pelvis is tilted or posture is imbalanced, the piriformis tightens abnormally and compresses this nerve.

3. Herniated Disc vs. Piriformis Syndrome

Category Herniated Disc Piriformis Syndrome
Pain Focus Back + Leg Buttock + Leg
Bending Forward Numbness worsens Little change in pain
Features Pain when coughing Worst when sitting
