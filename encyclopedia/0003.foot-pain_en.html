1. Could I have Plantar Fasciitis?

General foot fatigue and plantar fasciitis have different pain patterns. Recall the sensation of your first step in the morning.

  • Sharp, tearing pain near the heel when taking the first step after getting out of bed.
  • A tingling sensation in the arch when standing up after sitting for a long time.
  • Pain slightly decreases while walking but starts throbbing again in the evening.
  • Intense pain when pressing firmly on the inner part of the heel bone.

2. The Real Cause of the Pain

The plantar fascia is the core tissue that supports the arch of the foot. While being overweight, having flat feet, or wearing ill-fitting shoes are causes, fundamentally, pelvic misalignment creates uneven leg lengths, placing excessive load on one foot.

3. Bodyall's Total Care Solution

Bodyall Clinic not only removes inflammation in the sole but also corrects whole-body balance to prevent recurrence.

4. Lifestyle Tips to Prevent Recurrence

Practice these two daily habits to enhance the effect of your clinical treatment.

  • Calf Stretching: Pushing against a wall to stretch your calves significantly reduces tension on the plantar fascia.
  • Golf/Tennis Ball Massage: Gently roll a ball under the arch of your foot while sitting.
