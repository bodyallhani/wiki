1. Why Treat the Neck When the Head Hurts?

Occipital Neuralgia occurs when the greater or lesser occipital nerve is compressed along its pathway ascending to the back of the head. These nerves all emerge from between the upper cervical vertebrae (C1, C2). If the space between the neck bones narrows or alignment is disrupted due to turtle neck or straight neck syndrome, the nerves become physically crushed. The 'pain as if eyes are popping out' or 'tingling sensations in the back of the head' felt by patients are actually not brain issues, but electrical overload signals originating from the neck.
