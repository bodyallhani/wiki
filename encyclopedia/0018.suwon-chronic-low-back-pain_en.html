1. Why are back injections not a fundamental solution?

Temporary injection treatments used when back pain occurs are effective at blocking pain signals but do not change the physical alignment of a misaligned spine. On the contrary, if you engage in strenuous activities while unable to feel pain, degeneration within the spine may progress more rapidly.

2. Why is Bodyall SART correction necessary?

Our spine is an organically connected structure. When the pelvis is misaligned, the lumbar spine twists, causing chronic tension in surrounding ligaments. Bodyall's SART (Spinal Alignment Restoration Therapy) corrects the spine's Central Axis to a normal state, improving structure so that the back can be naturally supported without excessive muscle tension.

3. Chronic Low Back Pain Self-Diagnosis Checklist

  • Pain occurs at a specific angle when bending or straightening your back.
  • Your back feels stiff and your posture slumps after sitting for a long time.
  • You find yourself relying on painkillers or injections whenever you have pain.
