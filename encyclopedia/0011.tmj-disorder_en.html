TMJ & Facial Self-Diagnosis

"Clicking sounds when yawning—ignoring jaw pain can cause facial asymmetry"

💡 30-Second Key Summary from Bodyall

1. Jaw clicking and pain may signal upper cervical misalignment, not just a simple habit.

2. Precise structural diagnosis of the jaw-neck relationship is vital for fundamental recovery.

3. Bodyall restores the correct jaw axis via SART/Chuna integrated correction + Acupotomy.

1. TMJ Health Status Checklist

Many patients visiting Bodyall Suwon complain of jaw pain accompanied by headaches.

  • I hear 'clicking' or 'grating' sounds in front of my ear when opening/closing my mouth.
  • My jaw joint feels stiff or painful when chewing food.
  • My mouth doesn't open fully (locking) or opens in a zigzag motion.
  • My jaw muscles feel as hard as a rock upon waking up in the morning.

2. Mechanism: Why does the jaw twist?

The TMJ rotates around the upper cervical spine (C1, C2). At Bodyall Clinic, we analyze that the root cause of TMJ disorders is often the collapse of this 'axis' due to Turtle Neck or Straight Neck. Using the jaw while the axis is misaligned causes the disc to wear down or slip out of place, resulting in sound and pain.

3. Bodyall's Total Care Solution

From jaw pain caused by traffic accident trauma to chronic dysfunction, Bodyall solves the root problem through full-body alignment.

① Specialized Care: Integrated Jaw-Neck Correction

• SART / Chuna Therapy: Realigns the upper cervical spine—the root of the TMJ—to create an environment for smooth movement.
• Acupotomy (Adhesion Release): Minimally separates tight masticatory muscles and adhered soft tissues to normalize the range of opening.

② Korean Medicine Basis: Inflammation & Muscle Relief

• Comprehensive Solution: Acupuncture and Bee Venom Therapy rapidly remove joint inflammation, while moxibustion improves local blood flow.
• Modern PT: ICT and TENS relax fine muscles around the jaw and stabilize nerve pain.

4. Daily Habits to Prevent Recurrence

  • Discard Bad Habits: Resting your chin on your hand, biting nails, or chewing on only one side is detrimental to the TMJ.
  • Cervical Hygiene Stretch: Jaw health starts with neck alignment. Practice interlocking your hands behind your neck, opening your chest, and tilting your head back.
