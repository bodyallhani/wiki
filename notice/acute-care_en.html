[Precautions for the Acute Phase]

⚠️ Common characteristics of the acute phase:
  • The pain is sharp and very intense.
  • There may be little to no pain relief immediately after treatment.
  • Unless it is a chronic relapse, the duration of intense pain is relatively short.

During the acute phase, BodyAll doctors often advise, "Please come back tomorrow" or "Contact us immediately if the pain spikes again." Until the acute phase passes, please strictly follow these care instructions for each area.

1. Managing Acute Lower Back Pain

This is often caused by acute disc damage or herniation. Muscles are in a state of excessive tension to protect the damaged spine. Avoid forced extension (bending backward) or lying flat in ways that increase pain, as this can worsen the condition.

Step A. Lie flat on your stomach, place your forehead on the back of your hands, and breathe comfortably. The key is to relax without applying force to your lower back or arching your back.
Step B. If you feel no pain in position A for 5-10 minutes, gently lift your head. If comfortable, support your upper body slightly by resting on your elbows.
Step C. If there is no discomfort in position B, place a cushion under your chest and rest comfortably. ※ If you feel any pain during any step, return to the previous step immediately.

2. Managing Acute Neck and Shoulder Pain

Similar to the back, excessive backward tilting of the neck can irritate the nerves during the acute phase. You may perform the BodyAll Stretching with your hands interlaced behind your neck, but be extremely careful not to "snap" or force your neck backward.
