[Lumbar Cushions / Choosing a Good Chair]

1. Proper Use of Lumbar Cushions

  • The cushion must be placed firmly against the deepest part of your lower back (behind the navel).
  • Avoid pushing your hips too far back or arching your back excessively while leaning against the cushion.
  • The key is to relax comfortably against the cushion, not to force a lumbar curve.
  • Instead of a cushion that is too thick or hard, choose one with moderate elasticity that naturally supports your back.
  • You should be free of tension throughout your body while your spine remains in a proper, upright position.

2. How to Choose a Good Chair

💡 A Word from BodyAll:
"There is no such thing as a special 'good chair.' A chair that doesn't strain your spine is simply a good chair."
  • A good chair is one where 'Pelvic Rolling' occurs naturally without extra effort.
  • It should provide the same supported feeling as when using a lumbar cushion.
  • Recommended Approach: Master the use of lumbar cushions and pelvic rolling first, then sit in the chair yourself to choose.
