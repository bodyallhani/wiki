[BodyAll Stretching (Spinal Alignment)]

⚠️ Caution:
The key is NOT to force your lower back or neck to snap backward. Do not apply excessive force to spread your shoulders. Focus on the sensation of your upper chest expanding vertically while your upper back gently arches backward.
💡 Why is it called 'Spinal Alignment'?

This stretching is more of a 'technique' to finely adjust the position of your vertebrae rather than simple muscle relaxation. Effectiveness is maximized when you focus on the movement of the bones rather than the pulling sensation of the muscles.

  • Essential during treatment: Recommended 100+ times a day.
  • Lifelong maintenance: 10-20 times a day is beneficial even after recovery.
  • Frequency over intensity: Doing it briefly but frequently throughout the day is key.
