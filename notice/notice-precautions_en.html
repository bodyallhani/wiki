[Essential Post-Treatment Precautions] ★

1. Consistency is the key to successful correction.

Spinal correction relies on continuity. If treatment intervals exceed one week, your body tends to revert to its previous misaligned state. If you find it difficult to keep your appointments, please inform the clinic in advance. (Treatment can be performed daily depending on your symptoms.)

2. Temporary discomfort may occur initially.

This is a common occurrence during the very early stages of treatment (1st or 2nd session). As the spine and muscles, which have been stiff and misaligned for years, loosen up and adapt to the new alignment, you may feel some pain. This is a signal that your body is changing, not that the treatment was incorrect.

3. If pain is severe, visit us before your next appointment.

If you feel significant discomfort, do not endure it; it is better to visit the clinic earlier than scheduled. Prompt action allows for safer and more efficient correction. (We recommend making a same-day appointment to minimize waiting time.)

4. The period when pain decreases is the most critical!

Reduced pain does not mean a complete cure; it is a sensitive stage where healing has just begun. If you let your guard down and overexert yourself now, misalignment can easily recur.

5. Feel free to ask our medical staff anything.
Spinal Correction Effect = ‘Sufficient Number of Adjustments’ + ‘Time Spent in the Corrected Position’

The goal of BodyAll Clinic is to fix not only the misaligned bones (hardware) but also the habits that maintain them (software). Ensure that your spine adapts well to the correct position by resolving any questions.

6. We strive for "Spinal Hygiene."

Just as skipping tooth brushing leads to cavities, failing to maintain "Spinal Hygiene" leads to disease. The knowledge and practices provided by BodyAll will become lifelong habits. Please integrate these spinal hygiene methods into your daily life.
