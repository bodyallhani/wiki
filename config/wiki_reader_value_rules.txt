These are internal writing and review rules, never patient-facing content.
Write the direct answer early, connect the patient's actual difficulty to the confirmed clinic actions, then give only the research detail needed for the decision. A correct research summary alone is insufficient. Show why the specific confirmed actions matter to this question without inventing clinic protocols or comparative efficacy.
Do not print source-availability or authorship explanations such as '제공된 연구', '이번에 확인한 자료', '이 글에서는 기준을 만들어 설명하지 않습니다', '특별한 프로토콜을 뜻하지 않습니다'. If a fact is missing, omit the unsupported claim. Never insert a second disclaimer to defend an already accurate clinic fact or correctly scoped patient-count statistic.
Research limitations that change interpretation stay beside the finding once. Do not omit an unfavorable study conclusion. Do not repeat it in the direct answer, a separate section, summary and FAQ. A short article may have no FAQ or summary. Do not use fixed section counts or length as quality signals.
Absence of timing data cannot establish that time since surgery/childbirth is irrelevant, that movement findings alone establish treatment safety, or that treatment should replace natural recovery. Generic active/passive movement observation does not validate a diagnosis or distinguish tissue causes. Correct the premise; do not compensate with a disclaimer.
For prognostic studies, preserve association vs causation and adjustment limits. Do not infer obstetric or child-related factors are irrelevant from nonsignificance. Do not translate combined low-back/pelvic populations into isolated SIJ disease. For treatment reviews, match each result to its actual intervention and population; the postpartum PGP subgroup's exercise/education results are not direct evidence about manipulation, Chuna or SART.
Patient education may describe what information to bring without claiming a clinic-specific protocol. If no source supports a suggested clinical next step, do not infer it merely from absence of evidence. Keep emergency guidance actionable, appropriate to anatomy and supported by its linked source.
Use one relevant existing article link when it materially advances this reader's next question, without re-answering that page. An authoring/status card says only '의학적 검토 대기(PENDING)' and the generation date until actual clinician approval; do not reintroduce '작성자', 'AI 편집', '자동 작성 초안'.
