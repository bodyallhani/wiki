1. The Real Cause of Lumbar Discs: "A Tilted Floor"

A lumbar disc herniation is a consequence. When the pelvic foundation collapses, the spine above it must twist to maintain balance.

  • Pelvic Imbalance: When the ground (pelvis) tilts, the body bends the spine in the opposite direction to compensate for the center of gravity.
  • Asymmetric Pressure: Excessive pressure is applied to only one side of the disc between the curved spinal segments, causing the nucleus pulposus to protrude.

2. Muscle Pain and the Leaning Tower of Pisa Principle

When your back hurts and muscles become rock-hard, it’s not necessarily a muscle disease; it’s a 'desperate effort to support the bones.'

  • Muscles as Ropes: If a building leans, ropes (muscles) must pull hard from the other side to keep it from collapsing.
  • Screaming Muscles: Muscles holding onto bones 24/7 eventually become inflamed and develop adhesions. Standing the Tower (bones) vertically is a more fundamental solution than just massaging the ropes.
