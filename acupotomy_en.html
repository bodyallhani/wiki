"If relief is only temporary, the problem is likely 'Adhesion'"

Chronic lumbar discs, stenosis, and frozen shoulder often involve 'Adhesion'—where tissues become stuck together and harden. Body-all Clinic focuses on restoring 'Structural Freedom' by using Acupotomy, a precision conservative therapy that releases these adhesions at the source.

💡 Q. Why is chronic pain so resistant to standard physical therapy?

Because soft tissues act like 'glue,' holding your bones and nerves in a misaligned position.

Chronic inflammation causes muscles and ligaments to adhere firmly to the bone[1]. In this state, general adjustments won't last because the adhered tissue pulls the bones back out of alignment. Body-all’s Acupotomy Adhesiolysis breaks this cycle, creating the optimal environment for your spine to heal.[2]

2. When is Acupotomy Essential?

"Adhesion is more than stiffness; it's a structural barrier."

  • Relieving intense pain caused by tissues pulling on the periosteum of the bone.
  • Treating Frozen Shoulder where range of motion is severely limited.
  • Alleviating Stenosis where the spinal canal is narrowed by thickened ligaments.
  • Managing Post-Surgery Syndrome caused by scar tissue adhesion.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Q. How is Acupotomy different from standard acupuncture?

While standard acupuncture focuses on muscle relaxation, Acupotomy uses a specialized flat-bladed tip to physically release hardened 'adhesions' that pull on the periosteum and restrict joint movement.
