💡 Q. Why are chronic conditions like stenosis, frozen shoulder, or old discs so difficult to treat?

A. It's because the surrounding soft tissues are stuck together like 'glue,' holding the bones tightly.

Chronic inflammation or repetitive injuries cause muscles and ligaments to adhere and harden. In this state, no matter how much you try to align the bones, the adhered tissues will pull the bones back into the misaligned position. Body-all uses Acupotomy Adhesiolysis to break this cycle of tough adhesions, first creating an 'environment' where the spine can return to a healthy position.

2. Why Acupotomy is Essential

"Adhesion goes beyond simple stiffness; it causes structural pain."

  • Adhered tissues undergo degeneration, causing the tissue itself to harden firmly.
  • When adhesions occur in muscles and ligaments, they create a force that pulls on the periosteum of the bone, causing intense pain.
  • This phenomenon increases pain by itself and acts as a primary culprit in worsening symptoms by increasing the load on muscles and joints.
