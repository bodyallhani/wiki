"If relief is only temporary, the problem is likely 'Adhesion'"

Chronic lumbar discs, stenosis, and frozen shoulder often involve 'Adhesion'—where tissues become stuck together and harden. Body-all Clinic focuses on restoring 'Structural Freedom' by using Acupotomy, a precision micro-surgery therapy that releases these adhesions at the source.

💡 Q. Why is chronic pain so resistant to standard physical therapy?

A. Because soft tissues act like 'glue,' holding your bones and nerves in a misaligned position.

Chronic inflammation causes muscles and ligaments to adhere firmly to the bone. In this state, general adjustments won't last because the adhered tissue pulls the bones back out of alignment. Body-all’s Acupotomy Adhesiolysis breaks this cycle, creating the optimal environment for your spine to heal.
