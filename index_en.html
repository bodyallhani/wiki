Pain and Spine Care Information

"Working toward an active life through spine care"
SART: connecting assessment with reassessment after treatment

Bodyall considers symptoms and medical history, spinal and pelvic palpation, active and passive range of motion, gait and posture when planning care. SART (Spine Alignment Restoration Therapy) is a Chuna care protocol that links work on load and movement, detailed alignment work, immediate reassessment through active movement, and guidance for daily activities.

Located in Ingye-dong near Suwon City Hall Station, the clinic offers weekday appointments until 8:30 p.m. Treatment choices and intensity depend on examination findings, changes in symptoms, previous treatment responses and individual risks.

This wiki helps readers find information about services and conditions. It does not provide an individual diagnosis or guarantee treatment results.

Acupotomy

An invasive procedure using a flat-tipped needle-knife to apply mechanical stimulation or attempt release at selected soft-tissue sites. Suitability and risks should be discussed individually after examination.

Bee Venom Therapy

Pharmacopuncture using purified bee venom. Allergic and systemic reactions are possible, so medical history, medicines and individual suitability need review.

BodyallFit Weight Management

A program to discuss weight-management goals and prescription suitability with the chief director. Eligibility for remote consultation and program details are explained individually.
💡 What happens at a first visit for pain or spine care?

The clinician reviews symptoms and medical history, palpates the spine and pelvis, and assesses active and passive range of motion, gait and posture. Treatment and intensity are chosen according to the findings. After treatment, movements that brought on symptoms and the range of motion are checked again.
