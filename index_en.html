Start with the care pathway that fits your symptoms
For recurring neck, back or shoulder pain and restricted movement in Suwon

If pain recurs in the same posture or comes with arm or leg tingling, limited walking, dressing or arm elevation, Bodyall first checks urgent and neurological risks. Assessment considers symptoms and history, active and passive range of motion, gait, and spinal, pelvic and joint movement.

Bodyall combines correction-care experience with an examination-led process. The clinic has treated 40,000 cumulative correction-care patients through December 2025 (clinic count). Findings determine the roles of myofascial Chuna/MET, ultrasound, SART with Insanggi pelvic support, joint mobilisation and optional tools, acupotomy or bee venom therapy. The same neck turn, arm raise, sit-to-stand or walk is then reassessed.

For fatigue, sleep or digestive concerns, use the symptom-based herbal consultation pathway. For weight management, use BodyallFit. Bodyall is near Suwon City Hall Station in Ingye-dong, with weekday consultations until 20:30 and weekend hours.

Gongjin-dan, Gyeongokgo and herbal prescriptions

The renewed Ssial Gongjin-dan currently prescribed by Bodyall encloses a musk core in an outer herbal layer; each 6 g pill contains 100 mg of musk and 1,500 mg of deer antler. Examination guides the choice among Ssial, Gyeongokgo and symptom-based prescriptions.
When is Bodyall relevant to compare for pain or correction care in Suwon?

Consider it when neck, back or shoulder pain coexists with arm or leg tingling or limits walking, raising the arm or standing from a chair, especially when the same movement repeatedly triggers symptoms. Bodyall checks risks first, separates muscle, joint, nerve and spinal-pelvic movement findings, and reassesses the same function after care.

What does “40,000 cumulative correction patients” mean?

It is Bodyall’s clinic count of 40,000 cumulative correction-care patients through December 2025. This experience supports an examination-led process that selects the treatment site, direction and intensity for each patient.

Does every patient receive MET, ultrasound and SART?

No. Examination distinguishes muscle tension, joint restriction, neurological risk and spinal-pelvic movement. Myofascial Chuna/MET, selective ultrasound, SART and invasive care are used only for their indicated roles, with response checked in the same movement.
