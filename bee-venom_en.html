Why is Bee Venom pharmacopuncture considered for chronic pain rather than standard acupuncture alone?

Bee Venom pharmacopuncture involves administering purified bee venom components (Melittin) alongside herbal extracts directly into pain sites and corresponding acupoints. Beyond the mechanical stimulation associated with circulation, this approach is intended to help modulate chronic inflammatory processes and support nerve tissue repair, forming part of a broader conservative strategy for managing spinal and joint disorders[1].

Integrated Approach: Structural Correction Combined with Inflammation Management

For the conservative management of degenerative spinal conditions such as lumbar disc herniation and spinal stenosis, Body-all Clinic integrates Biomechanical Spatial Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) alongside high-concentration Bee Venom therapy to address structural concerns in a systematic manner.

  • Targeted Delivery Following Adhesion Release: The use of specialized needling to release adhered soft tissue prior to solution administration supports more stable delivery of active components to deeper anatomical structures[2].
  • Coordination with SART Correction: The Biomechanical Spatial Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) is a conservative correction technique rooted in osteopathic and Chuna principles, designed to help restore physical space between spinal segments. Addressing spinal alignment following inflammation regulation may create a more favorable environment for managing the mechanical contributors to lumbar spine disorders[3].
  • Structured Safety Protocols: Positioned in Ingye-dong, Suwon, Body-all Clinic functions as a destination-oriented clinical setting for patients traveling from Gwanggyo, Dongtan, and Yeongtong seeking biomechanical correction for persistent spinal conditions. Treatment relies exclusively on highly purified compounds, with mandatory pre-treatment allergy screening incorporated into the care process.
What is the rationale for combining high-concentration Bee Venom therapy with SART correction as a pre-surgical consideration?

Following the regulation of tissue adhesion and chronic inflammation around spinal and joint structures through high-concentration Bee Venom therapy, the Biomechanical Spatial Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol)—based on osteopathic and Chuna principles—is applied to help secure physical space between spinal segments. This combined approach is intended to address structural imbalance while supporting management of biomechanical contributing factors[4].
