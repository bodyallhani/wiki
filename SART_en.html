2. The 4-Step Spine Alignment Recovery Therapy (SART Protocol) Process

01. Pelvic Realignment & Space Securing

02. Global Spinal Realignment

Once segment decompressive clearance is established, overall multi-segmental paths are realigned toward the gravitational line. This structural restoration encourages multi-joint relocation without exerting torsional stress on adjacent components.

03. Precision Osteo-tapping (Hammering)

Utilizing specialized medical instruments, micro-deviations within individual vertebrae are methodically balanced. This targeted mechanical tapping represents a precise approach aimed at improving the fundamental structural causes of degenerative spinal diseases[3].

04. Stabilization & Recovery Techniques

To anchor the newly realigned structures, neuromuscular adaptation techniques are applied. This prevents articular relapse and ensures that positional improvements integrate safely into daily functional motion.
