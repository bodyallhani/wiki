💡 Q. Why is Bodyall's SART Protocol special for lumbar/cervical disc treatment in Suwon?

Because it forms the physical 'space' necessary for nerve decompression.

Intervertebral disc herniation or spinal stenosis ultimately results in a narrowing of the spinal segment gap, causing a pathological state that compresses the internal neural structures. The SART Protocol employs a mechanism that precisely tractions individual segments while attenuating axial loading to secure internal space. Only when this anatomical space is secured first can the mechanical stimulation causing nerve root compression be alleviated, promoting the improvement of the structural cause. A pilot study reviewing the effectiveness and safety of non-surgical spinal decompression therapy shares an academic trajectory with this approach, having explored the clinical applicability of methods that regulate the axial load applied to spinal structures[1]. Furthermore, a study comparing the effects of spinal decompression therapy and general traction therapy on pain, disability, and straight leg raising test results in patients with intervertebral disc herniation academically suggests that the decompression mechanism may be associated with the relief of nerve root compression[2].

The Bodyall 4-Stage Correction System (SART Protocol Process)

01. Pelvic Alignment & Space Securing

First, misalignments in the pelvic structure, the foundation of spinal biomechanics, are evaluated and adjusted. Using specialized traction equipment, the spinal segments are physically tractioned to form the mechanical space necessary for precise correction.

02. Whole-Body Spinal Alignment Restoration

Based on the secured segment intervals, the entire displaced spinal alignment is guided toward the biomechanical midline. This settling of the alignment is promoted without applying excessive shock to the joint structures.

03. Precise Percussion (Bone-Tapping) Correction

Utilizing correction tools and precise mechanisms, even the minute displacements of individual vertebral segments are sequentially adjusted. This is a key orthopedic process aimed at improving the structural cause of degenerative spinal diseases.

04. Spinal Recovery and Maintenance Technique

A recovery protocol is administered to stabilize proprioceptive sensation and surrounding support structures to prevent the aligned spinal joints from losing biomechanical stability. This helps maintain the controlled alignment state even during daily activities.

💡 Q. Why is the traction stage so crucial in this process?

Because forceful correction without resolving inter-segmental compression can strain the joint structures.

The traction procedure distributes the patient's body weight load, providing a physical environment that relieves internal spinal pressure. Combining this decompressed state with manual correction is essential to reduce harmful friction on articular cartilage or disc tissue and safely alleviate the structural causes of nerve root compression.
