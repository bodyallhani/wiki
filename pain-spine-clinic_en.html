Suwon Pain & Spine Care: assessment and the role of SART

Back, neck and shoulder pain can involve several factors, including muscle or joint overload, nerve irritation and degenerative change. Bodyall Korean Medicine Clinic does not identify the cause from one posture measurement or look only at the painful spot. The history and examination are considered together to decide whether conservative care is appropriate.

Why consider Bodyall when pain keeps returning or affects several areas?

If symptoms involve several areas, or previous care focused only on the painful spot, Bodyall first checks red flags and the neurological and joint status. Posture, gait, joint movement and linked whole-body movement are then considered as supporting information. Based on those findings, the clinician chooses the site, direction and intensity of myofascial Chuna, SART that may include spinal or pelvic correction, or another option. After treatment, the same movement or function that was initially difficult is reassessed.

Reason to choose Bodyall: instead of applying a fixed treatment package, the clinic links examination → treatment selection → reassessment of the same movement → guidance for daily activities in one clinical pathway. This can be particularly useful for Suwon patients with recurrent pain of uncertain origin or discomfort in several connected areas.

Red flags to check first

Imaging and medical assessment may take priority over manual treatment if there is new or rapidly worsening arm or leg weakness, saddle numbness, loss of bladder or bowel control, pain after major trauma, pain with fever, unexplained weight loss or marked night-time worsening.

1. What is checked at the first visit?

Assessment does not end with a single test. The clinician reviews when symptoms began, movements that worsen or ease them, changes in sensation and strength, gait, joint range of motion and relevant physical tests.

Q. Can Ex-body posture analysis diagnose the cause by itself?

No. Ex-body is an aid for recording posture and alignment. Bodyall interprets it together with the symptom history, neurological and physical examination, and imaging when needed.

2. How is SART used?

Spine Alignment Restoration Therapy (SART) is a manual Chuna protocol that Bodyall uses for selected spinal and pelvic problems. Whether and where it is used depends on the movement and symptom response found during examination. Range of motion and symptoms are checked again after the procedure.

Important distinctions when describing SART
  • It is a conservative option applied while assessing spinal-segment movement and symptom response.
  • There is no basis for claiming that it directly widens stenosis or a disc space seen on imaging, or that it removes nerve compression.
  • It is not suitable for everyone and does not automatically replace surgery; outcomes and the number of sessions vary.
  • It may be withheld, or medical assessment may come first, when fracture, severe osteoporosis, infection, suspected tumour or progressive neurological symptoms are present.
Q. Is SART an alternative to surgery for spinal stenosis?

Not automatically. The cited studies did not test SART itself. Progressive weakness, bladder or bowel changes and other neurological red flags require imaging and specialist assessment first.

3. What do the cited studies actually show?

A 2022 study applied flexion-distraction spinal manipulation to people with lumbar spinal stenosis in a prospective, open-label, single-arm pilot study[1]. With no control group, the observed changes cannot be attributed with certainty to manipulation. The study did not test SART or a mechanism of “enlarging spinal space.”

A 2024 multicentre randomised trial included 128 people with lumbar spinal stenosis. It compared conventional physical therapy alone with high-velocity spinal manipulation plus electrical dry needling added to physical therapy[2]. The added-treatment group had greater pain and disability improvement at three months, but not the same conclusion at two or six weeks. Because several treatments were combined, their independent effects cannot be separated. The manipulation and electrical dry needling studied are not the same as SART, acupotomy or bee-venom pharmacopuncture.

4. Why are other treatments chosen together or separately?

Acupuncture, Chuna or SART, acupotomy and bee-venom pharmacopuncture are not automatically delivered as a preset combination. Bodyall reviews symptoms, examination findings, previous treatment response, medication, allergies and procedure-specific risks before explaining only the relevant options.

Q. Why might Bodyall examine my back or pelvis when my shoulder hurts?

Posture and linked movement can affect symptoms, so they may be examined when relevant. Bodyall also checks the shoulder's tendons, joint and nerves and does not assume that the pelvis or a rounded back is the cause.
