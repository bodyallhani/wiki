Why check my back and pelvis if my shoulder is the one hurting?

Because the human body is organically connected like a chain.[1] For example, when the pelvis is misaligned, the back rounds as a compensatory mechanism, forcing the scapula forward. In this rounded shoulder state, the shoulder muscles are stretched and overloaded, meaning that treating only the shoulder yields temporary results. Body-all Clinic precisely targets this structural causality to treat the root cause of shoulder pain, based on a conservative care protocol considering the entire kinetic chain.[2]

Why is SART treatment effective at the pre-surgical stage?

SART (Space Spine Alignment Regulation Therapy) is a biomechanical conservative realignment technique based on the principles of traditional osteopathy and Chuna manual therapy to secure physical space between spinal segments.[3] Because it reduces nerve compression and intradiscal pressure caused by structural deformation, aiming to improve the fundamental structural causes of degenerative spinal diseases such as chronic low back pain or spinal stenosis, it serves as a safe and reasonable alternative at the pre-surgical stage.[4]
