This is because the human body is connected as an organic chain from head to toe. For example, if the pelvis is misaligned, the back rounds and the scapula is pushed forward as a compensatory action. 💡 Q. Why is Biomechanical Spatial Spinal Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) effective as a pre-surgical option?

Biomechanical Spatial Spinal Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) is a biomechanical conservative correction technique that secures physical space between spinal segments based on the principles of traditional osteopathy and Chuna manipulation. A prospective pilot study examining the influence of flexion distraction spinal manipulation on patients with lumbar spinal stenosis indicates that conservative approaches aimed at creating physical space between spinal segments have been an area of academic interest in managing degenerative spinal conditions[1]. Because it aims to reduce nerve compression and disc pressure caused by structural deformities, thereby improving the fundamental structural causes of chronic degenerative spinal diseases such as lumbar back pain and spinal stenosis, it becomes a rational and safe alternative to be considered prior to surgical intervention.
