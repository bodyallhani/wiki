1. Technical Features of Body-all Chuna

While space-securing techniques are the core value of SART, Body-all's Chuna contains equally sophisticated correction skills.

  • Segmental Correction Handling Each Bone:
    This isn't just about pushing or pulling the overall line of the spine. We accurately locate the specific segment causing the pain and help it return to its proper place.
  • Safe Osteo-tapping Technique:
    We use safe medical tools to precisely adjust micro-misalignments that are difficult to reach by hand. This is a modern application of the "Osteo-tapping" principle popular for spinal health.
  • Recovery of Joint Range of Motion:
    By normalizing the movement of joints that have become stiff due to misalignment, we relieve pain and restore the body's natural flexibility.
