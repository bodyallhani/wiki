Core Technologies of Body-all Chuna

  • Spatiotemporal Spinal Realignment Therapy (SART) Integration:
    When managing conditions like herniated discs, SART is utilized as a conservative corrective technique that secures physical space between spinal segments, based on the principles of traditional osteopathy and Chuna manual therapy. It presents a highly structured solution aimed at improving the fundamental structural causes of degenerative spinal diseases.[2]
  • Precise Manual Segmental Realignment:
    The clinician manually palpates the misaligned vertebrae and targets only the specific segments out of the normal range to optimize biomechanical function.
  • Modern Osteo-tapping (Golta):
    For deep segments or micro-displacements difficult to reach by hand, we utilize safe medical tools to deliver precise structural realignment.
  • Customized Mobilization Techniques:
    We perform gentle mobilization to restore joint range of motion naturally according to the patient’s body type and flexibility.

Indications Requiring Structural Realignment

Acute Back Pain/Stiff Neck
Sudden spinal sprains or restrictions in cervical mobility upon waking up.
Postural Imbalance
Observable stages of forward head posture, rolled shoulders, or pelvic asymmetry.
Early-stage Disc Conditions
Cases where localized stiffness and deep discomfort around the spine outpace radicular pain.
Postpartum Pelvic Care
Realigning the pelvic ring safely to recover optimal structural mechanics following childbirth.
