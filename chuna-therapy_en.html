Core Technical Components of Body-all Chuna Therapy

  • Biomechanical Spatial Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) Integration:
    For conditions such as herniated discs, the SART Protocol is applied as a biomechanical spatial decompression manual therapy designed to overcome the limitations of traditional osteopathic Chuna therapy by mechanistically expanding intervertebral spaces and neural foramina[3]. This structured protocol is aimed at improving the underlying structural mechanisms associated with degenerative spinal conditions[4].
  • Precise Manual Segmental Realignment:
    The clinician manually palpates each vertebral segment to identify displacement, targeting only the specific misaligned units that deviate from normal biomechanical range to optimize functional restoration.
  • Modern Osteo-tapping (Golta) Technique:
    For deep spinal segments or micro-displacements difficult to access manually, calibrated medical instrumentation is utilized to deliver precise, controlled structural realignment.
  • Customized Joint Mobilization:
    Gentle mobilization techniques are performed to restore natural joint range of motion, individually tailored according to each patient's body type and flexibility profile.

💡 Q. Why is SART treatment considered effective in the pre-surgical stage of spinal disc herniation?

The Biomechanical Spatial Decompression Chuna Therapy (SART Protocol) mechanistically applies controlled biomechanical decompression to expand the intervertebral space, addressing structural narrowing that contributes to nerve root compression[3].

By focusing on structural mechanisms rather than temporary symptom relief, this segmental correction approach offers a systematic conservative pathway equipped with objective assessment protocols, allowing patients to explore non-surgical options before considering invasive intervention[4].
