💡 Why does spinal correction heal internal diseases?
All nerves connected to the internal organs pass through the spine. When the spine is misaligned, interference occurs in the nerve signals traveling to the organs, leading to autonomic nervous system issues such as indigestion, Hwa-byung (fire illness), and palpitations. Bodyall aims for fundamental internal medicine treatment by securing space between vertebrae to normalize nerve flow.
