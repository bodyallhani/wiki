How Bodyall structures care choices

Rather than attaching every symptom to one label or one treatment, the patient’s records are connected to treatment selection and a repeatable reassessment target.

1

Check what comes first

Review onset, aggravating and relieving factors, previous diagnoses and tests, medicines and supplements, and warning signs. If testing comes first, Korean medicine care is not placed ahead of it.

2

Separate treatment roles

Herbal medicine is considered individually from the leading symptom, overall state, and current medicines. Chuna or SART has a separate role only with relevant musculoskeletal findings and pain or movement limits.

3

Use the same measures again

Compare symptom frequency and severity, sleep or meal records, and a target such as work, walking, or concentration under the same conditions to decide whether to maintain, adjust, or refer.

Urgent assessment comes before a Korean medicine consultation when there is

New or severe chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, one-sided weakness or slurred speech, vomiting blood or black stool, or persistent severe abdominal pain; unexplained rapid weight loss or high fever; suicidal thoughts or a severe change in awareness.

Questions this guide is designed for

DIGESTION & MEALS

SLEEP & TENSION

Track sleep timing, awakenings, caffeine, medicines, and when palpitations or tension occur, then set a repeatable reassessment target.

FATIGUE & FUNCTION

When fatigue limits daily function

Check whether fever, weight change, or breathing symptoms require medical work-up before defining a daily-function goal.

Herbal and musculoskeletal care do not have the same role

Symptom-specific herbal care

The leading and accompanying symptoms, constitution, meals, sleep, and current medicines inform suitability and cautions. We do not promise a response date or cure, or advise reducing prescribed medicine without the prescriber.

Chuna or SART when indicated

Considered after examination when separate neck, back, or movement findings coexist. It is not presented as opening spinal space to normalize organ nerves or as a root cure for internal disease.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bodyall treat indigestion or insomnia by correcting the spine?

We do not make that assumption. These symptoms have many possible causes, so previous diagnoses, medicines, and warning signs are reviewed first. Herbal care is considered for the symptom pattern; Chuna or SART has a separate role only when relevant musculoskeletal findings coexist.

Can I consult Bodyall while taking prescribed medicine?

Yes, but do not stop it on your own. Bring the medicine name, dose, reason for use, and test or diagnosis records to support interaction and priority checks.

How is progress reassessed?

The selected symptom frequency and severity, sleep or meal records, and the same daily function are compared under the same conditions. Worsening or unexplained change can trigger referral for appropriate testing.
