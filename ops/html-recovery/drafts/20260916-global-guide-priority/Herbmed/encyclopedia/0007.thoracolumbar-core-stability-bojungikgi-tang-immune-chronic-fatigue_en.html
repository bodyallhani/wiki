Persistent fatigue and lethargy despite sleep in Suwon: cause evaluation, herbal fit and trunk-function assessment

💡 Q. How does Bodyall assess fatigue that persists despite sleep together with low-back or trunk stiffness?

Bodyall does not reduce chronic fatigue to thoracolumbar alignment or ‘adrenal fatigue.’ We first review onset and duration, whether sleep is refreshing, worsening after activity, dizziness or cognitive change, medicines, caffeine, weight change and prior test results. Fit for Bojungikgi-tang–type or individualized herbal care is judged separately from back or trunk symptoms reproduced by a specific posture or movement.

When medical assessment comes first

New or severe chest pain, breathlessness, fainting, one-sided weakness or speech difficulty, persistent fever, bleeding, unexplained weight loss or rapid functional decline require medical assessment before herbal or corrective care. Persistent fatigue may also need evaluation for other causes such as anemia, thyroid disease, infection, medication effects or sleep disorders.

1. How Bodyall separates the clinical decisions

Safety triage, herbal selection and accompanying musculoskeletal care are decided step by step rather than forced into one mechanism.

Review

We document onset and duration, restorative sleep, post-exertional worsening and recovery time, dizziness or cognitive change, appetite and bowel pattern, medicines, supplements, caffeine and prior tests.

Separate

Persistent fatigue including possible ME/CFS is separated from anemia, thyroid, infection, sleep, medication and other causes. Medical testing or referral comes first when indicated.

Select

Appetite, digestion, sweating, low vitality, sleep, constitution and medicines inform whether a Bojungikgi-tang–type or individualized formula fits. Trunk care is selected only for movement findings.

Reassess

We track post-exertional worsening, recovery time, restorative sleep and daily function, not just a fatigue score. If musculoskeletal care is used, the same sitting, sit-to-stand or trunk rotation is retested.

Experience with accompanying back and trunk findings

Bodyall uses MET and ultrasound and had treated 40,000 cumulative correction-care patients through December 2025 (clinic count). This experience informs whether accompanying back or trunk findings need treatment and how the same movement is reassessed afterward.

2. Separate roles for Bojungikgi-tang, Chuna and SART

Bojungikgi-tang and individualized herbs

Prescription fit is judged from appetite, digestion, sweating, low vitality, sleep, bowel pattern, constitution and current medicines. The same formula is not used for every fatigue presentation, and prescribed medicines are not stopped without the prescriber.

Chuna and MET

When low-back or trunk discomfort is reproduced by prolonged sitting, rising or rotation, care may address joint motion and muscle or fascial function. It does not replace evaluation or care for ME/CFS, immune or mitochondrial disorders.

SART

For correctable musculoskeletal findings, SART addresses selected segmental motion. It is not described as decompressing neurovascular tissue to normalize microcirculation, ATP or immune axes, or as improving herbal delivery.

Referral and activity management

When another cause needs evaluation or post-exertional worsening is prominent, medical assessment and activity within individual limits come first. Goals and tracking are adjusted to function.

3. What the evidence does and does not show

Persistent fatigue and ME/CFS assessment

CDC states that no single confirmatory test exists for ME/CFS; history, examination and targeted tests are used to rule out other fatiguing illnesses. Impaired function with fatigue, post-exertional malaise, unrefreshing sleep and cognitive impairment or orthostatic intolerance are assessed together. (CDC).

Spinal manipulation and autonomic measures

A 2024 systematic review included 14 trials and 618 participants. Overall low-quality evidence found no alteration of autonomic measures, and it did not test chronic fatigue, microcirculation, immunity, ATP or Bodyall SART. (PubMed).

Review of Hochuekkito/Bojungikgi-tang

A 2021 paper reviewed basic pharmacology and varied clinical evidence in infectious, inflammatory and frailty-related contexts. It does not establish individual benefit for ME/CFS, mitochondrial ATP restoration, superiority with thoracolumbar correction or improved herbal delivery. (DOI).

4. Frequently asked questions

Is chronic fatigue caused by a misaligned low back or trunk?

That cannot be assumed. Duration, post-exertional worsening, sleep and other medical causes are reviewed first; back or trunk symptoms are assessed separately for movement-reproduced musculoskeletal findings.

Can I start Bojungikgi-tang immediately?

People with fatigue differ in appetite, digestion, sleep, constitution and current medicines. Fit is decided after consultation, and prescribed medicines should not be stopped independently.

Must herbs be combined with Chuna or SART?

No. Musculoskeletal care is selected only when back or trunk pain or movement restriction is present. The cited studies do not establish superiority of combined care.

What should I record before the first visit?

Record onset, sleep duration and restoration, when worsening begins after activity and how long it lasts, daily function, dizziness or cognitive change, medicines and prior test results.
