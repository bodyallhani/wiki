Shallow Sleep and Night Palpitations in Suwon: Cause Screening, Herbal Fit and Neck-Function Assessment

💡 Q. How does Bodyall assess shallow sleep, nighttime palpitations and neck stiffness together?

Bodyall does not attribute insomnia to craniocervical alignment or vagus-nerve compression alone. We first review sleep onset and awakenings, snoring or witnessed apnea, caffeine and alcohol, medicines, anxiety, daytime function, and the timing and pulse pattern of palpitations. Fit for Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan–based or other individualized herbal care is decided separately from fit for Chuna, MET or SART when neck symptoms are reproduced by movement.

Signals that need medical assessment first

New or severe chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, a persistent rapid or irregular pulse, one-sided weakness, trouble speaking or a sudden severe headache require urgent assessment rather than a routine appointment. Loud snoring with witnessed apnea, severe daytime sleepiness, worsening depression or thoughts of self-harm also need appropriate sleep or mental-health evaluation.

1. A concrete reason to choose Bodyall

We separate cause screening, herbal selection and treatment of accompanying neck findings, then reassess the same sleep and function measures.

Check

We review sleep onset and maintenance, duration, wake time, naps, snoring or apnea, caffeine and alcohol, medicines, mood, palpitations and daytime function.

Differentiate

Sleep apnea, restless legs, thyroid or cardiac problems, medication effects and chronic insomnia are distinguished. The palpitation pattern may call for ECG or other medical testing first.

Select

Palpitations, anxiety, dry mouth, appetite and digestion, bowel pattern, constitution and medicines guide whether Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan–based or another tailored prescription is suitable. Neck care is selected only for relevant movement findings.

Reassess

Sleep-onset latency, awakenings, total sleep time, morning restoration and daytime function are compared using the same record. If the neck is treated, the same rotation and flexion task is retested.

Scope of verified operating facts

Bodyall performs MET and ultrasound. The cumulative 40,000 correction-patient figure is counted through December 2025. It is not a count of insomnia patients or an outcome, cure or effectiveness rate for Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan or SART; it describes experience selecting and reassessing care for accompanying neck and back findings.

2. The roles of herbal and neck care are separate

Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan–based or tailored herbal care

Palpitations, anxiety, dry mouth, sleep pattern, digestion, bowel pattern, constitution, prescriptions and supplements are checked before deciding fit. One formula is not used for every case, and prescribed medicines are not stopped without the prescriber.

Sleep behavior and referral

Wake time, naps, caffeine, time awake in bed and a sleep diary are reviewed. Evidence-based care such as CBT-I and indicated testing or referral remain part of the decision for chronic insomnia.

Chuna and MET

These may address joint, muscle and fascial function when pain or stiffness is reproduced with neck rotation or flexion. They do not replace evaluation or treatment of insomnia or palpitations.

SART

Bodyall’s correction protocol may address motion at selected segments when correctable musculoskeletal findings are present. We do not claim that it releases vagus-nerve compression, restores slow-wave sleep or improves herbal delivery to the brain.

3. What the evidence does and does not show

Chronic insomnia care

The American Academy of Sleep Medicine guideline strongly recommends multicomponent cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) in adults with chronic insomnia. Herbal assessment should therefore include sleep behavior, comorbidities and access to indicated standard care. (PubMed).

Upper-cervical manipulation study

The 2015 preliminary cross-over study measured immediate HRV, blood pressure and pain after manipulation in 10 healthy volunteers and 10 patients with acute neck pain. It did not test insomnia, slow-wave sleep, vagus-nerve compression, cerebral perfusion, Bodyall SART or combination with herbal medicine. (PubMed).

Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan review

The 2019 systematic review included 14 studies and 1,256 participants, but the included studies had low methodological quality and adverse-event reporting was insufficient for firm efficacy or safety conclusions. It did not clinically establish specific 5-HT or GABA mechanisms, direct extension of slow-wave sleep, or synergy with cervical correction. (PubMed).

4. Frequently asked questions

Does neck misalignment compress the vagus nerve and cause insomnia?

That cannot be assumed. Sleep pattern, apnea, medicines, cardiac and thyroid causes are checked first, while neck symptoms are assessed separately as a musculoskeletal problem reproduced by movement.

Can I start Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan right away?

People with insomnia differ in palpitations, anxiety, dry mouth, digestion, constitution and medicines. Fit is decided after assessment, and prescribed medicines should not be stopped without the prescriber.

Must herbal medicine and SART be used together?

No. Musculoskeletal care is selected only when neck pain or motion restriction is present. The cited studies did not test superiority of combined care.

What should I record before the first visit?

Record bedtime and wake time, sleep-onset latency, awakenings, caffeine and alcohol, snoring or apnea, timing and pulse pattern of palpitations, and current medicines.

An assessment that separates insomnia causes, herbal fit and neck care

Bring a sleep record, the pattern of palpitations and any motion-related neck symptoms so the order of testing, herbal care and musculoskeletal treatment can be decided.
