1. The "Tension Principle" of Shoulder Pain

  • The Pulling Hand (Cause): A rounded back forces the scapula forward, stretching shoulder muscles to their limit.
  • The Tearing Fabric (Result): Muscles under tension suffer high friction during movement, leading to inflammation (Frozen Shoulder) or damage (Rotator Cuff Tear).
  • Body-all's Solution: Instead of just patching the tear, we let go of the pulling hand (back realignment). This is the only way to prevent re-tearing.
