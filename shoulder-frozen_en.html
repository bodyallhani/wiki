        💡 Clinical FAQ & Biomechanical Insights                 
            Q. Why does shoulder pain persist despite treatment?            

A. It is because the shoulder muscles are moving in a 'Tightly Pulled Tension State.' Moving your arm while your back is hunched is like rotating your arm while someone is tightly grabbing your sleeve from behind. Body-all prioritizes straightening the rounded back to release this structural pulling force first.

       
                
                
            Q. What is the biomechanical effect of rounded back correction on shoulder pain treatment?            

An increased thoracic kyphosis causes the scapula to protract anteriorly, which brings the tension of the rotator cuff muscles to a critical threshold. SART correction at Body-all Clinic aims to eliminate this structural tension, thereby reducing joint friction and deep inflammation, helping to restore the normal range of motion of the shoulder.[1]

       
                
                
            Q. What role does acupotomy adhesiolysis play in frozen shoulder treatment?            

Acupotomy is applied to the hardened joint capsule segments caused by adhesive capsulitis to precisely release chronic adhesions. This is a safe biomechanical conservative treatment alternative available in the pre-surgical phase, contributing to the gradual recovery of joint mobility.[2]
