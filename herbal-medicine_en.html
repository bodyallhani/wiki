What to discuss before choosing Gongjin-dan or Gyeongokgo

  1. Describe fatigue and consider other causes

    Note its onset, whether rest helps, and how long you can work, study or walk. Unexplained fatigue lasting several weeks or affecting daily life needs medical assessment before choosing a tonic. Symptoms may warrant investigation for other causes, including anaemia, thyroid disease or diabetes.

  2. Discuss examination findings and the prescription

    Bodyall considers constitution, pulse findings, energy and current symptoms when deciding whether to prescribe. Even if you have a product in mind, share sleep, digestion, medicines, allergies and previous test results. Confirm the prescription name, ingredients and amounts, form, directions and duration.

  3. Compare the same daily activities

    Briefly record fatigue, sleep, eating and the daily activity you find most difficult before use. Report changes using the same measures, together with new discomfort, when discussing the next prescription or dosing plan.

  4. Start with the published amount, quantity and price

    Compare the stated musk amount and per-pill price for Gongjin-dan and the stated 30-pack quantity and price for Gyeongokgo. Confirm the actual prescription, total cost and collection date after the examination. For a gift, first establish the intended recipient’s health conditions and medicines.

Questions about herbal consultations

Is Gongjin-dan or Gyeongokgo better for me?

You can first compare the published musk amount and per-pill price for Gongjin-dan with the published 30-pack price for Gyeongokgo. Fatigue alone does not determine the prescription; Bodyall also considers constitution, pulse findings, current symptoms, medicines and other conditions.

Does the Ssial method or its patent mean better absorption or results?

A manufacturing description or patent certificate alone does not establish superior absorption, fatigue relief or safety in patients. The Ssial form helps describe the product; ingredients, amounts and examination findings also matter when choosing a prescription.

Can I use herbal medicine with my current medicines?

List all prescription medicines, non-prescription medicines and supplements. Possible interactions need checking; do not combine them on your own or stop an existing medicine. Tell the clinician about pregnancy, breastfeeding, liver or kidney disease, planned surgery and allergies.

How Gongjin-dan research informs consultation

A small 2018 crossover pilot enrolled 23 healthy men under restricted sleep. In the overall crossover analysis, the primary cortisol difference was not significant and fatigue scores showed a trend towards improvement. Some fatigue and sleep measures improved over placebo in the first treatment period before crossover. [1]

A 2024 randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial recruited 103 adults with unexplained chronic fatigue lasting at least six months and analysed 90. At week 4, the Gongjin-dan group improved on the SF-36 Social Functioning score compared with control; no laboratory abnormalities or serious intervention-related adverse events were reported. These studies evaluated specific trial preparations, rather than directly comparing Bodyall’s Ssial Gongjin-dan or its compounding method. [2]

Signs to assess before a tonic consultation

Seek assessment for fatigue with unexplained weight loss, pauses in breathing or severe snoring during sleep, palpitations or breathlessness. Sudden chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or fainting requires emergency assessment. Stop the herbal medicine and contact a clinician if new problems such as rash, severe vomiting or jaundice develop.

Herbal ingredients can cause adverse effects and interact with medicines. Check before use if pregnant, breastfeeding, living with liver or kidney disease, or preparing for surgery.
