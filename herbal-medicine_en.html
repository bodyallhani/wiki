Suwon Gongjin-dan & Gyeongok-go:
Authentic Musk Restorative Formulas Prepared to Medical-Grade Standards

When evaluating Gongjin-dan or Gyeongok-go for systemic vitality restoration, the critical differentiating factors are the preparation method and the pharmaceutical grade of the raw herbs used. At Body-all Clinic in Ingye-dong, Suwon, both restorative formulas are compounded under an hGMP-certified framework using authentic musk to ensure that the final product functions as a genuine therapeutic prescription rather than a general wellness supplement.

Core Summary: Three Pharmaceutical Standards Applied to Body-all Herbal Prescriptions

Authentic Musk Verification Only KFDA and CITES-certified authentic musk is selected, with the precise prescription dosage disclosed transparently.
Volatilization-Resistant Core Method To minimize the loss of highly volatile musk compounds during storage, a core-condensation technique concentrates the active constituents at the center of the pill.
hGMP Medical-Grade Herbal Sourcing Concentrations of core ingredients such as deer antler and ginseng are precisely controlled, with lower-grade food-agricultural substitutes strictly excluded from the formulation.
💡 Why does the pharmaceutical grade of musk affect the therapeutic efficacy of Gongjin-dan?

Under Korean regulatory classification, herbal preparations containing musk must be manufactured as a licensed pharmaceutical rather than a general food product, since musk itself cannot legally be included in food-grade formulations. This distinction is clinically significant because the bioactive constituents in authentic musk have been associated with circulatory and metabolic support in traditional pharmacological literature[1]. Body-all Clinic uses only KFDA-certified authentic musk (regulated under CITES) and prepares Gongjin-dan with a concentrated deer antler content, while Gyeongok-go is compounded exclusively from herbs that have passed multiple hGMP quality assessments—reflecting a measurable pharmaceutical-grade standard compared to non-pharmaceutical alternatives.

Core-Condensation Method to Prevent Musk Volatilization: The "Seed" Preparation Technique

The term "Seed (芯)" refers to the innermost core of the pill—conceptually analogous to a seed or embryonic center—reflecting the manufacturing intent to concentrate the musk densely at this central point in order to minimize evaporative loss of its active compounds during the drying and storage process.

A 72-Hour Traditional Steaming Process, Reformulated for Modern Convenience: Body-all Gyeongok-go

Following the classical preparation method documented in the Donguibogam—which specifies a 72-hour steaming process within a porcelain vessel—Body-all Gyeongok-go has been reformulated into an individually packaged stick format that preserves the traditional compounding standard while enabling portable, on-the-go administration.

  • Individually Packaged Stick Format: Eliminates the inconvenience of measuring doses from a traditional jar; the portable design is optimized for professionals and students with demanding schedules.

Distinguishing Prescription-Grade Herbal Medicine from Food-Grade Supplements

Unlike general food-category products, pharmaceutical-grade herbal formulations undergo rigorous KFDA certification procedures that govern raw material sourcing, dosage transparency, and manufacturing standards.

  • Ingredient Transparency: Musk content is disclosed transparently across two product tiers—an Original formulation (100mg) and a Practical formulation (40mg).
  • Diagnosis-Based Prescription: Licensed practitioners provide individualized prescriptions based on a patient's specific constitutional assessment and health status.
