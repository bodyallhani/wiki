Frequently asked questions

Must I always avoid a food listed for moderation?

Use the recommended and moderated categories to guide meals. Discuss major changes in light of allergies, conditions and nutrition.

Can MBTI or these questions confirm my constitution?

Use MBTI to reflect on preferences and follow your professional constitution assessment for constitution guidance.

What if symptoms after food or activity keep returning?

Record timing, food, portion and activity for your visit. Do not dismiss severe or persistent symptoms as a constitution response.

Using this guide

Put the food and lifestyle guidance for your assessed constitution to use. Discuss medications, allergies and conditions with your clinician.
