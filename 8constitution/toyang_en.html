Frequently asked questions

Must I always avoid a food listed for moderation?

No. It is an example from a traditional classification, not a personal prohibition. Consider allergies, conditions, nutrition and your response; discuss broad restrictions with a clinician.

Can MBTI or these questions confirm my constitution?

No. They are prompts for reflection, not an assessment or validated one-to-one map.

What if symptoms after food or activity keep returning?

Record timing, food, portion and activity for your visit. Do not dismiss severe or persistent symptoms as a constitution response.
