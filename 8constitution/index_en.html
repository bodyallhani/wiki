About MBTI

MBTI can start a conversation. It cannot diagnose a constitution or provide a validated one to one mapping. Do not restrict your diet based on personality or a test alone.

Using this guide

This guide helps you use an existing constitution assessment in daily life. Food examples describe a traditional classification; they are not individual prescriptions or proof of treatment effects. Discuss medical conditions, allergies and medicines with your clinician.
