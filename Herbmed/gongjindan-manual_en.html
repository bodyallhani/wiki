01 · DAILY ROUTINE

Start with these four steps

Even if your prescribing doctor has explained the routine, review these steps once more. Follow any individual instructions you were given in preference to this general guide.

  1. Take out tomorrow’s pill the night before.

    One unopened pill may stay at room temperature overnight. Keep the rest refrigerated and away from direct sunlight, heaters and hot cars. You may take it straight from the refrigerator, but it is easier to dissolve at room temperature.

  2. Drink a glass of water before your morning pill.

    Moisten your mouth, then let one pill dissolve slowly. You may brush your teeth and clean your mouth before taking it.

  3. Notice the change in flavor and aroma at the core.

    You may first taste deer antler, honey and Cornus fruit, followed by a more distinctive, spreading aroma. This is the core containing the musk. Firmer pieces may be grouped together or separated. You may chew them; with time to spare, let them dissolve slowly too.

  4. After finishing, wait about 30 minutes before brushing.

    This is Bodyall’s recommended routine for allowing you to experience the lingering aroma. Count from when the pill has completely dissolved. You may eat immediately after finishing it.

Missed your morning dose?

Before lunchTake one pill in the same way about 30 minutes before lunch.
Not until evening?Taking it before dinner may be an option, but a late dose can interfere with sleep in some people. If you sleep lightly or feel alert for a long time after taking it, ask your prescribing doctor first.
Missed a whole day?Return to your usual prescribed dose the next day. Do not take two pills to make up for a missed dose.

A consistent time helps you maintain the routine and compare your response. Missing one dose does not mean all previous changes have been reset.

A routine that fits a busy morning

You may clean your mouth before work and dissolve the pill slowly while seated doing light tasks. Avoid driving, operating machinery or vigorous exercise with a pill in your mouth.

02 · GENUINE NATURAL MUSK

Understanding your prescribed Gongjindan

Is an online ‘Gongjindan’ the same as your prescription?

Buying a food product presented as ‘Gongjindan’ differs from receiving natural-musk Gongjindan after a doctor’s assessment.

Online and social media listings may use similar names and packaging. Look beyond the appearance: check the product category, actual ingredients and natural-musk content. Civet, sometimes marketed with wording suggesting musk, is a different material from natural musk obtained from musk deer.1

The ‘Ssial’ design places musk at the center

Ssial Gongjindan was designed to reduce the loss of aromatic musk components during preparation and storage and make careful use of this valuable material. The musk is formed into a small central core, then enclosed in a mixture containing deer antler, Angelica root and Cornus fruit.

04 · RESEARCH & INGREDIENTS

What have studies examined?

Gongjindan research has examined fatigue together with sleep, quality of life and daily functioning. These are also useful areas in which to observe your own response.

Fatigue and sleep under sleep restriction

A 2018 randomized, placebo-controlled crossover study assessed fatigue, sleep and stress-related measures in healthy men whose sleep was restricted.5

Chronic fatigue and quality of life

A 2024 randomized controlled study assessed fatigue scores, quality of life and social functioning in patients with chronic fatigue.6

Studies use different Gongjindan formulations and doses in different participants. They do not directly compare Bodyall Ssial Gongjindan Wonbang and Silsok, but can help explore what effects the ingredients in Gongjindan may have.

Timing and method

Do Wonbang and Silsok require different methods?

Use the same method. Their musk content differs, so do not take extra Silsok pills to imitate Wonbang. Follow your prescribed quantity.

What if I miss the morning?

Take one pill the same way about 30 minutes before lunch. Check whether you have already taken it to avoid a duplicate dose.

Can I take it before dinner if I miss lunch too?

This may be an option. If you remain alert for a long time or sleep lightly, avoid late dosing and discuss the timing with your prescribing doctor.

Should I take two pills after missing yesterday?

No. Resume your usual prescribed quantity today.

Must the firm core dissolve completely?

You may chew it. It can feel like one firm piece or several pieces. If you have time, let it dissolve slowly.

I do not notice the core or its aroma clearly.

Saliva and how you dissolve the pill can affect the sensation. Aroma strength alone does not show content or response. If you are concerned about the product, contact us with a photo of the packaging.

I swallowed it. Should I take another?

Do not take another. Next time, let it dissolve slowly.

Taking it on an empty stomach feels uncomfortable.

Do not force yourself to continue on an empty stomach. Tell your prescribing doctor about the discomfort and any existing digestive problems so the timing can be adjusted.

Brushing, food and coffee

Can I brush before taking it?

Yes. You may brush and clean your mouth first. Afterwards, we recommend waiting about 30 minutes from finishing the pill before brushing.

I brushed immediately. Did I lose the effect?

Do not assume the effect is lost or take an extra pill. Follow the recommended interval next time.

Can I eat immediately afterwards?

Yes, once the pill has fully dissolved. Remember the brushing interval after finishing.

When should I drink water?

We recommend a glass of water before taking it in the morning. If dissolving it is uncomfortable, do not force it; ask us.

Can I drink coffee?

You may drink coffee. If you notice palpitations or insomnia, reduce or avoid coffee and tell your prescribing doctor. Pay particular attention if you are sensitive to caffeine.

Storage and daily life

Should I keep it refrigerated?

Keep it refrigerated routinely. One sealed pill for the next day may be left at room temperature overnight. Follow any specific storage instructions on the packaging first.

Can I take it straight from the refrigerator?

Yes. It may be easier to dissolve when it has reached room temperature.

How do I carry it to work or on a trip?

Carry only the quantity you need in its packaging, away from direct sunlight, hot cars and heaters. Ask about storage duration if refrigeration will be unavailable for a long time.

Can I dissolve it while working?

You may do light work while seated. Avoid driving, operating machinery or vigorous exercise with a pill in your mouth.

Should I only take it on important meeting or competition days?

Follow your prescribed schedule. Avoid trying it for the first time on an important day or increasing the dose yourself. Observe your response and sleep beforehand.

Can athletes subject to doping tests take it?

Consult your team’s medical staff and prescribing doctor before taking it. Doping-test compatibility cannot be assured without reviewing the ingredients and competition rules.

Can I share it with family?

This medicine was prescribed for you. A family member should have their health and current medicines reviewed separately before taking it.

Response and consultation

I did not feel anything special after the first pill.

Compare the daily difficulties you started with—morning fatigue, concentration, activity and sleep—rather than expecting one strong sensation. Share your notes at the agreed review time.

Will a larger dose work faster?

Do not increase it yourself. Have your prescribing doctor adjust the quantity and duration.

How long should I take it?

Follow your prescribed duration. At the end, review your response and goals to decide whether to continue or adjust.

Can I take other medicines or supplements with it?

Provide a list of prescription medicines, over-the-counter medicines and supplements. Have your prescribing doctor review them, especially medicines for chronic conditions.

What about pregnancy or breastfeeding?

Tell us about possible pregnancy, plans for pregnancy, pregnancy or breastfeeding. Do not start or continue without an individual review.

I have palpitations or cannot sleep.

Pause further doses and report the dosing time, coffee intake and symptoms to your prescribing doctor. Severe chest pain, fainting or difficulty breathing requires urgent medical care.

Should I tolerate discomfort as a ‘healing reaction’?

Do not assume discomfort is a healing reaction. Stop and consult your doctor to review the dose, timing or prescription.

I have a rash or possible allergic reaction.

Stop taking it and contact us promptly. Swelling of the lips, tongue or throat or difficulty breathing requires emergency care. Tell us about existing allergies beforehand.

What information helps at follow-up?

Your version, start date, dosing time, missed days, fatigue score, daily changes, sleep, coffee intake, other medicines and any discomfort.
