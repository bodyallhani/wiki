Herbal medicine, Chuna and SART at Bodyall

Bodyall Korean Medicine Clinic offers care that may combine Korean herbal medicine with Chuna and SART (Spine Alignment Restoration Therapy), according to the symptoms being assessed. If sleep, digestive or fatigue symptoms occur alongside neck, back or lower-back discomfort, consultation can include both herbal care and assessment of spinal and pelvic movement. SART is the manual spinal-care protocol used at Bodyall.

Consultation considers what each treatment is intended to address, whether there is a reason to use them together, and how changes in symptoms and daily function will be reviewed. The combination is not applied to every symptom; necessary tests or other specialist care may take priority. Findings from separate herbal-medicine and manual-therapy studies do not by themselves establish an added benefit from combining the treatments. Each article distinguishes the scope of its evidence.
